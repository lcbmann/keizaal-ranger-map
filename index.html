To the Rangers of Skyrim

This atlas exists so no range is watched by memory alone. Mark roads, shelters, threats, caches, contacts, patrol routes, and reports worth sharing. Keep entries truthful, discreet, and current.

A Ranger should know their own range, but the Corps is stronger when that knowledge is passed on. Walk, watch, listen, remember, and report.

No land unwatched.
Ranger-Captain Lorn Valecroft

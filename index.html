Atlas Instructions

Using the Field Atlas

Choose what you need to do, then follow the short steps. Your atlas saves automatically in this browser.

1

Add Entries

  1. Choose Mark, Trail, or Range.
  2. Place or draw on the map.
  3. Select every category that fits the entry, then fill in confidence and notes.
  4. Use the checkmark beside the entry to keep it.
2

Find Entries

  1. Use Search, Mapped By, and the category filters.
  2. Select an entry in the Atlas list to zoom to it.
  3. Hold Ctrl, Cmd, or Shift while selecting to choose several entries.
  4. Press Esc to clear active filters.
3

Share Entries

  1. Share Atlas creates a code for the entries you choose, or a ready-to-post Field Report.
  2. Receive Atlas lets you review an official copy, Skyrim references, or another Ranger's code before adding anything.
  3. Save Image downloads the current visible map as a PNG.
4

Clean Up

  1. Undo reverses your most recent change.
  2. Scrape Clean removes chosen categories from custom entries, Skyrim references, or the entire map.
  3. Use Undo immediately if you remove the wrong set.
